sonic the hedgehog 3

Sonic the Hedgehog 3 is a 2024 film where Sonic, Knuckles, and Tails team up to face a new threat: Shadow, a mysterious hedgehog with unique powers. To stop him and save the planet, they must form an unexpected alliance. The film features Keanu Reeves as the voice of Shadow. It's the third installment in the Sonic movie franchise, following Sonic the Hedgehog (2020) and Sonic the Hedgehog 2 (2022).

spider man no way home

Spider-Man: No Way Home focuses on Peter Parker's life after his identity as Spider-Man is revealed, causing chaos and bringing dangerous villains from other universes into the MCU. Desperate for help, Peter seeks Doctor Strange's assistance to make the world forget his identity, but the spell goes wrong, shattering the multiverse and unleashing familiar foes. Peter must then confront these villains, discover what it truly means to be Spider-Man, and protect his loved ones and the multiverse.
